Privacy Policy for Smart Roll

Effective Date: October 2, 2026 • Developer: Dart Nexus Lab • Package: com.dartnexuslab.smartroll

Dart Nexus Lab ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Smart Roll mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, handle, and safeguard your data when using our geotagged QR attendance services.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data

  • Account Details: Full Name and University Email Address.
  • Academic Identifiers: Student Roll Number, Degree Program, Department, Batch, Semester (for students).
  • Professional Title: Academic Designation (for faculty members).

B. Device & Hardware Permissions

Camera Access (CAMERA): Used strictly in real-time to scan time-sensitive attendance QR codes via on-device ML Kit. Video feeds or camera frames are never stored, saved, or uploaded to any remote server.

Location Access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Used strictly in the foreground during attendance submission to verify physical presence within the classroom geofence. Background location is never accessed or monitored.

2. Purpose of Processing

We process your data to:

  • Verify student and faculty identity for institutional records.
  • Eliminate attendance fraud and proxy marking using cryptographic validation.
  • Calculate attendance metrics, semester eligibility, and course analytics.

3. Security & Anti-Fraud Architecture

Smart Roll employs SHA-256 rolling cryptographic signatures and anti-replay nonce tracking. Every validated attendance record is linked to a tamper-evident audit hash.

4. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted Google Cloud infrastructure:

  • Firebase Authentication: Identity management.
  • Cloud Firestore: Encrypted database storage.
  • Google Play Services: Core platform support.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

In-App Self-Service Deletion: Users can permanently delete their account, credentials, and attendance records at any time directly inside the Smart Roll app by opening the top-right menu (or tapping Account Settings & Data Deletion), choosing Delete Account, confirming their password, and confirming deletion. All Firestore documents, personal attendance logs, and Firebase Auth credentials are deleted immediately and irreversibly.

Email Deletion Request: Users may also request complete deletion of their account and associated data by emailing dartnexuslab@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Upon identity verification, all records will be permanently purged within 30 days.
